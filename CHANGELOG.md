# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

The first release.

- Changes and History: your uncommitted changes and any commit's diff, unified
  or split, live as you save. Huge diffs show their first screen at once.
- Staging by file, hunk, or line; commit, amend, and undo the last commit.
- Branches: switch, create, and a branch diff against main.
- Fetch, pull, and push through your own git.
- Folders of several repositories, with an all-repositories view.
- Recent projects in the title (⌥⌘O).
- A built-in terminal with tabs.
- AI commit messages from free models on OpenCode Zen, Vercel AI Gateway, and
  OpenRouter, falling back to another free model when one is busy.
- Onboarding that sets up an AI provider and your first project.
- Configurable keyboard shortcuts.
- Errors that say what to do next.
